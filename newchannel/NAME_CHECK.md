## Recommendation: **dotsim**

- **One word for both languages.** "dotsim" is pronounceable in English and Spanish ("dot-sim"), so the EN channel and
  the ES-419 audio track share one brand, one handle, one wordmark. No translation, no second identity to build.
- **Free and ownable.** @dotsim returned 404; no channel, app or trademark of that name was found. A coined word is the
  strongest kind of mark and is easy to search ("dotsim traffic" will find us; "tiny systems traffic" finds five others).
- **It says the promise.** dot + sim: every video is a simulation of dots. It already contains "dot", our logo.
- **The wordmark works.** `dotsim` in mono type has one **i**; its dot is the moving agent, and the "o" of "dot" is a
  second dot the agent can visit. 6 letters: reads at icon size on a phone.
- **The approved idea survives as the tagline**: EN *"Tiny systems, explained with dots."* · ES-419
  *"Pequeños sistemas, explicados con puntos."*

Risk: it sounds a bit technical. The tagline, the companion dot's face and warm narration carry the warmth; the name only
needs to be memorable and unique. If the Producer prefers something more poetic: **Ten Thousand Dots / Diez Mil Puntos**
(both handles free), with the wordmark rule adapted to the "i"s in "Diez Mil" and the full stop in EN.

Before launch: claim @dotsim (YouTube), check the same handle on TikTok/Instagram/X and the domains dotsim.com/.tv
(not checked here), and run the free USPTO/EUIPO search for "DOTSIM" in class 41 (entertainment/education).

# New channel (Claude as master brain) · concepts v1 — 2026-10-12

Brief from the Producer: Claude and its agents design and run a second YouTube channel freely (concept, brand, logo,
wordmark, scripts, sound, length). Constraints found by the scouts (`secondquest/docs/research/2026-10-12_SCOUT_SYNTHESIS.md`):
everything must be producible on CPU with free/permissive tools; YouTube demonetizes templated mass production, so each
video needs its own thesis and original visuals. Our edge: Claude writes code that draws anything, researches and
fact-checks, and composes sound in code — no character art and no credits needed.

## Three concepts
| | A · **Tiny Systems** (recommended) | B · Maps That Don't Add Up | C · The Price of Everything |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | "Why does this happen?" answered with **live simulations coded for each video**: why traffic jams appear from nothing, why the other queue is faster, how a rumor spreads, why cities grow in circles | geography oddities with animated maps: enclaves, weird borders, time zones (public-domain map data) | why everyday things cost what they cost, with kinetic infographics |
| Why it can win | visuals impossible to fake or template: every video is an original experiment; curiosity hooks; evergreen; Shorts come out of each sim | proven big niche | broad, searchable |
| Risk | needs strong storytelling so it is not a lecture | crowded by giants; maps look alike | data sourcing; crowded |
| Fit with Claude | **highest**: code is the art | high | medium |

## A in detail (proposal, to refine with the Producer)
- **Format:** 6-8 min long-form, one question, one simulation that the viewer watches "break" and then understands;
  a 40-60 s Short from every sim. EN first with ES-419 version (Spanish has less competition).
- **Look:** print-style system (inspired by what made the "World Brief" Short look rich): 3 inks + halftone + paper
  grain; agents are small round "dots" with tiny faces when the joke needs it. All code (Pillow/NumPy or Hyperframes).
- **Brand:** working names: *Tiny Systems* / *Pequeños Sistemas*, alt *Why It Happens*, *Simulado*. Wordmark: mono
  type where one dot of the "i" is a moving agent; the logo is that dot. The dot is the channel's companion (our rule):
  it reacts and lands in the wordmark at the hook.
- **Sound:** narrated (a voice the channel owns: designed with a commercial-OK open model, never a clone), sonified
  simulations (every collision/event is a soft musical tick, composed in code = a unique sound identity), no stock music.
- **Pipeline:** research + fact-check agents -> script (hook ends on the question + wordmark) -> sim prototype ->
  animatic -> style bible + pacing QC -> render -> packaging from outlier research.
- **Cost:** 0 credits per video (voice and visuals in code); optional thumbnails art only if a test proves it pays.

Next step after the Producer picks: name check (YouTube/handle availability), brand board (logo, wordmark, palette,
type), one pilot script + a 20 s sim test.

Rule: free sounds (engine synths, CC0, our own recordings) are used without asking. Sounds that cost credits
need the Producer's approval. Third-party game audio (rips) is never added here without a Producer decision
recorded in docs/<ep>/PRODUCER_DECISIONS.md.

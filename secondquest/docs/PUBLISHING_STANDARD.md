# SecondQuest: publishing standard (packaging and video)

**Status:** binding for every episode. Approved by the Producer on 2026-10-02.

**Sources:**
- MrBeast's production guide (*How to Succeed in MrBeast Production*);
- Paddy Galloway (MrBeast's YouTube strategist);
- YouTube's official help (thumbnails & titles, Test & Compare, hashtags).

**Reference thumbnail:** `docs/publish/EP001/thumbnails/SecondQuest_EP001_thumb_3.jpg`.

**Related standards:**
- Thumbnails in detail: `docs/THUMBNAIL_RULES.md`.
- Quest identity and QC: `docs/QUEST_V1_PROMPT_SPEC.md`.

Only claims backed by those sources are rules here. Nothing comes from unverified search snippets.

---

## 1. Packaging comes first (MrBeast, Galloway)

- **No episode goes into production until its title and thumbnail exist.** Both are decided together at the script stage, and the video is built to deliver on that promise.
- **Packaging gets real time,** not leftovers. Galloway: top creators spend about 30% of their effort on the idea and the packaging.
- **The metrics that matter:**
  - click-through rate (CTR), which comes from the thumbnail and title;
  - average view duration;
  - average percentage viewed.

## 2. Thumbnail

The full rules are in `docs/THUMBNAIL_RULES.md`. In short:
- Quest's face is big and in the left third, with **one extreme emotion**.
- **One** hero subject that pays off the promise.
- **One to three extreme words** that do not repeat the title.
- Fredoka Bold, white and yellow, thick outline.
- High contrast, readable at phone size.
- **Three variants for Test & Compare.**
- The image is generated without text; Claude adds the text. Quest QC runs before delivery.

## 3. Title

1. **Keyword first:** the game's name or the searched topic goes at the start, because the title is often cut off (YouTube).
2. **Aim for 50–60 characters.**
3. **Curiosity:** something familiar plus an unexpected twist (Galloway), e.g. "Farming Simulator: Why Millions Play a Game About Work".
4. **Extreme, intriguing wording** where it is honest (MrBeast: "I Survived" beats "I Spent").
5. **Accurate:** the video must deliver what the title promises.
6. **Write two titles** for Test & Compare:
   - **A**, searchable, published first;
   - **B**, curiosity, run as the test.
7. **ALL CAPS and emoji:** sparingly, or not at all.

## 4. Description

1. **The first two lines** carry the keyword and the hook, because they are what appears in search and under the video.
2. **A short summary** of what the video really covers.
3. **Chapters,** starting at 0:00, one per act. They are generated by `scripts/ep001-publish.py` (adapt the script for each episode).
4. **Call to action:** "What game deserves a 'why?' next?" plus "Subscribe and join the quest."
5. **Footer:** the upload default (subscribe line, channel tagline, business email).
6. **Hashtags:** 3 at most, at the end. Only the first 3 show above the title, and more than 60 are all ignored.

## 5. Tags

- **They carry little weight (YouTube).** Use the channel's base tags plus the game's name and two or three topic phrases.
- **Never** add other channels' names or topics the video does not cover.

## 6. The video itself

1. **Hook within the first seconds.** Most viewers drop off in the first minute (MrBeast). The question that opens the episode must appear right away.
2. **The thumbnail's promise is paid off early** in the video.
3. **Subtitles:**
   - English verbatim from the script, plus Spanish, uploaded as .srt files with timings;
   - auto-dubbing enabled.
4. **Leave room for the end screen** in the last 5–20 seconds.
5. **Technical:** 1080p30 is the maximum output and music is off by default.

## 7. Before publishing (checklist)

- [ ] Uploaded as **Private**.
- [ ] Thumbnail 1 (strongest) set; all three ready for Test & Compare.
- [ ] Title A set; title B ready for Test & Compare.
- [ ] Description with hook, chapters, CTA, footer and 3 hashtags.
- [ ] English and Spanish subtitles uploaded.
- [ ] Altered or synthetic content declared (AI voice and art).
- [ ] Audience: not made for kids.
- [ ] End screen set: subscribe plus a recommended video.
- [ ] Only then: switch to **Public**, and start Test & Compare once advanced features are available.

## 8. After publishing

- Check CTR and view duration in the first 24–48 hours.
- Read the Test & Compare result. YouTube reports the winner by **watch-time share**, usually within a few days to two weeks.
- Write down **one thing to improve** in the next episode (MrBeast: "improve something every time").

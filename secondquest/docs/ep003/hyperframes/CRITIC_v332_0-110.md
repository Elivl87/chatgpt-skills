### Findings (FIX)

1. **95.67–95.80: see-through kid (repeat of a Producer rejection).**
   - Source: `blockC/index.html` l.876, `#kidShot` opacity 0 → 1 over 0.32 s at `T.door + .28`.
   - At 95.70 and 95.77 the kid's body is transparent, with the hallway wall, door frame and picture visible through him.
   - frame_critic did not flag it because `#kidShot` holds no `data-role="character"` element. Please tag the kid image so the gate sees it.
   - Fix: hard cut on the door's full swing (≈95.7), or a 2–3-frame white bloom from the doorway light with the kid shot hard-cut underneath.
2. **Date continuity, 65.6–71.3 vs 71.33–72.6.**
   - The four phones open on a "MARCH" divider (l.546 `addDivider(ph, "MARCH")`), with "happy bday!!" and then "We should hang out."
   - Three weeks later the calendar says **OCTOBER 3 → 24**, and block B's source line says "early October".
   - Fix: make the divider "OCTOBER" (or "THU, OCT 1"). This is one string.
3. **Clock NOT IT, 86.53–87.87.**
   - The stamp's fill is 8 % opaque (`.stamp` background `rgba(255,240,230,.08)`). The hands, the "9" and the hub show through the letters, and the minute hand cuts across "IT" for the whole hold.
   - This is "object cut through by another object" on the beat's only word.
   - Fix: give the stamp a near-opaque paper fill (≈ .85) and keep the ink texture, or stop the hands before the stamp lands and place it clear of them.
4. **Map NOT IT, 85.50–85.67.**
   - The stamp lands at 85.52 and the shot cuts at 85.67, so it is readable for about 3 frames.
   - Through the same translucent fill, the Q/P pins sit over "T IT".
   - Fix: either drop it (the clock carries NOT IT on "time", and the map already makes its point when the pins meet), or land it about 0.35 s earlier with the opaque fill.
5. **Contrast ERROR: "Message" placeholder, 2.23:1** (check: C 35.25 and 44.21 → 97.0 / 105.97; on screen 88–92 and 102.5–106).
   - It is a 40+ px focal element. Darken it to about `#99948c` (the check's suggestion, 3:1 for large text).

## 5. Taste
Block C is the strongest idea-per-second stretch so far.
- **What works:**
  - the split-screen "compare";
  - the calendar that flips to the circled date;
  - a fever and a CLUNK as the two excuses;
  - a chat that literally flatlines;
  - two pins that meet when "distance" is denied;
  - the typed-and-deleted "want to hang o";
  - a door that opens on "Want to play?" and closes on "Nobody ever told us";
  - the $7.99 tag that lands where ASKING was.
- **What holds it back:**
  - The picture is clean. What lets it down is mostly the **sound**, which machine-guns every prop: 21 page flips, 16 ticks, 14 keystrokes, 4 identical stamps. That is exactly what the Producer rejected on Short #2.
  - The picture has three small craft slips: a see-through kid, a stamp you can see through, and a March that becomes October.

**The single change that raises it most:** cut C's sound to about nine motivated hits, so that the stamp, the flatline and the door actually land.

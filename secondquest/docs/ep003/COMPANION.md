## Why the fish
It is the alibi with eyes. The essay says nobody came for it, so it is the perfect deadpan witness: present in every act,
never looked at, reacting with one blink or an eye-roll while the friends do everything else. When the payoff says
"the fish was just the thing you could say out loud", the audience has been watching it all along.

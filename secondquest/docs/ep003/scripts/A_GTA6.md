# EP003 · Alternative A · Grand Theft Auto VI — "Before GTA 6"

Status: DRAFT v1 (not locked). Facts: only the "safe to say" list of `docs/ep003/research/02_dossiers_checked.json`.

## Creative spine
- **A. Viewer question:** Why is the whole world this obsessed with a game nobody has played?
- **B. Hidden question:** What does a thirteen-year wait do to the people waiting?
- **C. Surface attraction:** GTA is huge; the trailers are spectacular; hype.
- **D. Deeper desire:** a future everybody shares. In a world where nobody watches the same thing anymore, "GTA 6 is coming" was the one appointment everyone kept.
- **E. Thesis:** the wait stopped being empty a long time ago: it became a clock, a hobby and a shared place. We were not only waiting for a game; we were waiting *together*.
- **F. Payoff:** when the game arrives, we gain GTA 6 and lose "before GTA 6", the shared waiting room.
- **G. Risk:** a hype/news video (dates, trailers, prices) or "anticipation psychology 101"; also too close to EP002 if it becomes "you changed".
- **H. Recognition anchors:** "We got ___ before GTA 6"; two trailers watched frame by frame; the subreddit flair "Waiting for Nov 19th"; the same GTA V on three console generations; delays announced on earnings day.
- **I. Humor:** r/GTA6 was created before GTA V came out; the delay sentence "the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve"; a joke format that needed new world events every six months.
- **J. Why SecondQuest:** the essay is not about the game (nobody has played it). It is about the people waiting: the purest "why do we…" the channel has had.

## Master script (EN) — narration only

Since 2013, the world has had a pandemic. A new pope. A total solar eclipse. A congressional hearing about aliens.

And somewhere along the way, people started measuring all of it the same way.

Before GTA 6.

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for November 19th, 2026. Thirteen years after the last one. In that time, Rockstar has released exactly two trailers. And around a million people still check a forum about it every single day... for a game none of them has played.

Which is strange. Because waiting is supposed to be the empty part. The dead time between wanting something... and getting it.

But for millions of people, this wait was never empty.

Why?

It started as a joke. In July 2023, the day after a hearing about unidentified flying objects, a TikTok said: "We gonna have aliens before GTA 6."

And the internet never stopped.

Every time the world did something enormous, someone added it to the list. Pandemic. Eclipse. New pope. Artificial intelligence. Before GTA 6.

It sounds like a complaint about a video game. But look at what it actually does. It turns the absence of one game into a calendar for real life.

GTA V came out in September 2013. One writer pointed out that it was released six months after his son was born. By the time GTA VI was delayed again, that baby was in high school.

The game did not change. Everything around it did.

There is even a subreddit for GTA 6 that was created in 2012. A year before GTA V existed. Which means its members have been waiting for the sequel for longer than the previous game has been alive.

So the wait became a clock. But a clock just runs. Nobody runs a clock for fun.

And this wait was doing something else. People were not sitting still. They were busy.

Busy watching two trailers. Not twice. Hundreds of times. Frame by frame. Zooming into a billboard in the background. Building theories out of a reflection in a car window.

When the first trailer leaked early, Rockstar released it that same night. In twenty-four hours, it reached ninety million views on YouTube. A world record. For a minute and a half of a game nobody could buy.

On that subreddit, people wear their waiting like a badge. One flair just says: "Waiting for Nov 19th." Others: "Looking for the next trailer."

Fans learned to read Take-Two's earnings calls the way farmers read the sky. Because that is where the delays arrived. Each one politely explained that the extra time would bring "the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve."

Which is a very elegant way of saying: not yet.

When there is almost no information, people start producing it themselves. The theories. The memes. The countdowns. The anticipation stopped being something Rockstar gave the fans. The fans built it.

Waiting was not the delay before the hobby. Waiting was the hobby.

But why this game? People wait for sequels all the time.

Part of the answer is that GTA V never left. Take-Two says it has sold over two hundred and thirty million units. Across three generations of consoles. People bought it on one machine, then the next, then the next. The old game kept the light on while the new one was being built.

And part of the answer is what GTA promises. Not a level. Not a story. A whole place. A city you could disappear into for years.

Which creates a strange situation. Because with only two trailers, nobody actually knows what that place is like.

So for thirteen years, everyone has been playing their own version of GTA 6. In their heads.

And that version is perfect. It has no bugs. No server queues. No price. It is exactly as big as you need it to be.

The imagined game has had a thirteen-year head start on the real one.

Which brings us to the part nobody likes to say out loud. On November 19th, those two games finally meet.

But something else is about to change too.

Think about how rare it is now for everyone to wait for the same thing. Everyone has a different feed. A different show. A different algorithm deciding what matters to them.

Almost nothing is shared anymore.

But for thirteen years, almost everyone who plays games, and plenty who do not, agreed on one thing about the future. GTA 6 is coming.

It became a place people met. A joke strangers could finish for each other. A future everyone could see from wherever they were standing.

So... why did the wait become part of the game?

Maybe because, for a lot of people, it was the longest thing they ever did together.

"Before GTA 6" was never really about a game. It was a way of saying: a lot has happened to us. The world changed. We changed. And we were still here. Waiting. Together.

On November 19th, the waiting ends.

We finally get GTA 6.

What we lose... is "before GTA 6."

So what game have you been waiting for the longest? And what game deserves a "why?" next? Tell me in the comments.

Maybe that's our next quest.

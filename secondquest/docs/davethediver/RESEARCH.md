## 1. Release, studio and how it was made

- **Developer/publisher:** MINTROCKET (all caps on Steam), a small studio inside Nexon. [STEAM] [VGC25] VERIFIED.
  Nexon made Mintrocket a wholly owned subsidiary in **September 2024**. [WP] VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Credits:** director **Jaeho Hwang** (later Mintrocket CEO/head), producer Daehwon Kim, designer Chanhee Woo, programmer
  Bosung Seo, artist Kiyeop Chung; composers D'Anthoni Wooten and Jukio Kallio. Engine: **Unity**. [WP] VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Team size:** "around 30 developers", about one-third of a typical Nexon team (avg ~100; majors 200-300) [JA, Nov
  2022]. Later ~60 people at Mintrocket working on three new titles [Automaton]; Hwang calls a "20 to 30 people team"
  ideal and says it won't become "a huge 100 people team" [VGC, 18 Oct 2024]. VERIFIED.
- Nexon VP Kim Dae-won: "The reason Mint Rocket is operated in a small size is because we want to guarantee maximum
  creativity." [JA] VERIFIED.
- **Origins:** prototype "Dave" began 2017; announced in 2018 as a mobile game (NeoPle / Studio 42, with National
  Geographic), reannounced by Mintrocket May 2022. [WP] VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Inspirations (Hwang):** a seaside place on **Jeju Island** where the owner caught fish by day and cooked them at night
  ("The idea first came to me when I was on Jeju Island, which is kind of like the Hawaii of Korea") [PCG-INT, 25 Jul
  2023]; the Blue Hole draws on One Piece's "All Blue" [LevelUp]; *Torneko/Taloon's Great Adventure* (roguelike dives),
  *Abzu*, *Subnautica*, *Metal Gear Solid* (stealth, cardboard box), *Like a Dragon* (management + minigames) [PCG-INT]
  [WP]. VERIFIED.

## 3. Story and characters (FULL SPOILERS)

**Premise.** Dave is sunbathing on vacation when his friend **Cobra** phones about sushi and tells him to hurry. Dave
flies to the **Blue Hole**, a huge sea hole whose ecology and terrain change every time you enter, only to learn he must
catch the fish himself. Cobra has built **Bancho Sushi** for chef **Bancho**; Dave dives by day and works the restaurant
by night. [WIKI Dave/Cobra] [WP] VERIFIED (secondary).

**Characters**
- **Dave** — the player. Human scuba diver, brown hair and eyes; a big food lover (drops his holiday for sushi) [WIKI].
  Look in words: a short, round, bearded, middle-aged man in a wetsuit (visual description, VERIFIED only as "portly,
  meek, and ever reluctant to actually go on any adventures" [PCG-OP]). His name sounds like "broken toilet lid" in the
  Sea People language [WIKI]. Guest appearances: Balatro (Queen of Diamonds), Potion Craft, Fortnite skin [WIKI].
- **Cobra** — Dave's friend, investor behind Bancho Sushi; always chasing money by questionable methods; teaches the
  basics; runs a shop that buys Dave's non-fish items. Secretly **signed contracts hiring out Dave** to Dr. Bacon, Duff
  and reporter Udo without Dave knowing. First appeared in Nexon's *EVIL FACTORY* [WIKI]. "Former arms dealer"
  (meowdb) UNVERIFIED.
- **Bancho** — serious, obsessive sushi master who trained across Japan, never staying long. At the Dugong Hotel he
  served critic Lois Crawford a secret Whole-Roasted Shark Head, she left disgusted, he quit and co-founded Bancho Sushi
  with Cobra; the same dish wins over **Yoshie**, who then recruits staff. Name = Japanese "banchō", delinquent-gang
  leader. Star of the 2026 prequel *Bancho the Chef* [WIKI] [SIRUS Ch1]. VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Duff** — blond weapons expert and "avid otaku"; meets Dave by video call just after finishing season 2 of *Stra
  Stella*, a Gundam-style mecha + cute-girls anime. Builds weapons from Blue Hole materials (rope, scrap iron) [WIKI].
  Side quests: a sunken figure, "Leahs-chan", Duff's Dream Concert (rhythm minigame "Dream Live") [SIRUS] [WIKI].
- **Dr. Bacon** — archaeologist studying the Sea People; gives the iDiver app and a free suit upgrade (to 80 m), builds
  the radar and Sea People translator, briefly playable in "Escape the Pirates"; gets seasick [WIKI] [SIRUS].
- **Ellie** — biology grad student; asks for specimens, unlocks the **Ecowatcher** app; after Yawie she finishes her
  thesis and visits the bar [WIKI]. VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Sato** — Marinca card collector on a paddleboat; a parody of Pokémon's Ash (Satoshi) [WIKI].
- **Otto** — farmer; asks for childhood Moray Eel Curry, then builds the fish farm, rice paddy, vegetables, chickens;
  revealed as long-lost father of VIP rapper **MC Sammy** (he was shipwrecked for years) [WIKI].
- Others: **Yoshie** (recruiter), **Udo** (Weekly Fish reporter, FishMon app), **Vincent** (VIP who challenges Bancho),
  **Clara** (hunting the great white **Klaus**), **Maki** (speaks through a puppet), Michael Bang, Lois/Nolan Crawford
  [SIRUS] [WIKI]. Guests: Ichiban Kasuga, Majima, Godzilla, mxmtoon [WIKI].
- **Sea People** ("Low-Dwellers"): humanoid upper bodies, fish-like lower bodies, hidden for centuries in the Blue Hole.
  **Ramo** (Tenzhin's daughter), her guard **Suwam**, chief **Tenzhin**, Niamo and many villagers [WIKI] [SIRUS Ch2].
  "Mermaid" = these Sea People; no separate mermaid character found.

**Antagonists.** **John Watson**, leader of **Sea Blue**, poses as an ocean-protection group; really a front for illegal
fishing/dolphin poaching (TV Tropes via search, VERIFIED (secondary)). Fights Dave with a grenade launcher (Ch. 2), later
in a submarine; his sub is destroyed by a **Kronosaurus** (his fate: UNVERIFIED) [SIRUS Ch2/Ch6]. The **Gadon**: Sea
People twisted by experiments with the Divine Tree Fruit; can't be hurt, must be avoided [search summary]. Final boss
**Yawie**, a giant ancient anomalocaris, "mother of the hatchlings" [SIRUS Ch7].

**Arc by chapter** [GameBrief] [SIRUS]: Prologue "A Sushi Bar by the Blue Hole" (earthquake damages the bar) → Ch1
"Traces of the Sea People" (artifact at ~70 m; a **giant eye** watches; Giant Squid boss) → Ch2 "Into the Deep"
(Watson fight, rescue of Ramo, Wolf Eel, village found) → Ch3 "A Request from the Sea People" (earn trust) → Ch4
"Abandoned Cave" (Gadon released) → Ch5 "Melting Glacier" (pirates escape) → Ch6 "Glacier Zone" (stealth infiltration
of the Sea Blue base, MGS-style; poisoned beluga; Phantom Jellyfish, Helicoprion, Watson sub, Kronosaurus) → Ch7
"Broken Control Room".

**Ending.** The quakes and heating come from the **Divine Tree**: hatchlings cling to its roots/core. Dave fights Yawie
with a Duff-modded drone blaster ("Stella Laser", slows time); Tenzhin, Ramo and Suwam fire the great Sea People device
with a Divine Tree Fruit and kill Yawie. The waters heal, sick villagers recover [SIRUS Ch7]. Epilogue "Friends": all his
friends throw Dave a party at the bar; he goes to bed, dreams of space; the credits are a shooter where you blast the
developers' names (29 names, "Dev Killer" achievement); Dave is sucked into a wormhole; post-game teases "Let's Head to
the Jungle" [SIRUS Epilogue] [WIKI Minigames] [GameBrief]. VERIFIED (secondary). Who the Ch1 giant eye belongs to:
UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Core loop and mechanics

- **Two halves.** Day: dive the Blue Hole. Night: run Bancho Sushi. Hwang split them because a prototype mixing both
  "wasn't really that compelling because I couldn't focus on either task" [GDV]. VERIFIED.
- **Dive.** Oxygen is your health: it drains over time and in chunks when hit; below your suit's depth limit it drains
  much faster. Run out and Dave is hauled up keeping **one item** (first time free). Refill from tanks/clams; leave via
  surface or **escape pods** [TheGamer/GGRecon]. Weight limit via cargo box; overweight slows you [Sportskeeda].
  Layout and fish change every dive (roguelike influence) [PCG-INT] [STEAM]. Harpoon, found guns/melee weapons, nets,
  drones, ore mining, night dives with nocturnal fish [WP] [WIKI]. VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Bosses** (16 in wiki incl. DLC) such as Giant Squid, Wolf Eel, Truck Hermit Crab, Klaus, Lusca, Goblin Shark,
  Mantis Shrimp, Giant Gadon, Phantom Jellyfish, Helicoprion, Kronosaurus, Yawie, Ebirah [WIKI].
- **Night service.** Set the menu, serve dishes and green tea, clear plates, refill **wasabi** (no wasabi = no dishes),
  watch patience meters; enhance dishes (raises taste and price); earn **Artisan's Flame** to research recipes; hire,
  train and dispatch staff; VIP customers want special dishes; Cooksta followers rank the restaurant [WP] [NoobFeed]
  [WIKI Cooksta]. VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Phone apps** [WIKI]: iDiver (gear), Cooksta (restaurant social app), Marinca (cards), Ecowatcher, FishMon (photo
  jobs from Udo), Dive Log, Underwater Camera, GYAO! (Tamagotchi-style pet), plus minigames: Bouncy Dumbo, Leahs RUN!,
  Dream Live, EVIL FACTORY demo, Space Diver, Karaoke. Seahorse racing in the village Game Parlor [GDV] [WIKI].
- **Side businesses:** Otto's fish farm, rice paddy, vegetables, chickens; Gumo's seaweed farm in the village [WIKI].
- "Gulapaul": no such name found. UNVERIFIED (do not use).

- Bancho's emotional speech about his mission in the Prologue; Duff's anime obsession; Sato as an Ash parody; MGS
  stealth with rations and boxes; Dave's name joke [WIKI] [SIRUS] [PCG-INT].
- Crossovers as memes: Dave in Balatro, Fortnite, Potion Craft; Godzilla, Dredge, Ichiban Kasuga in Dave [WIKI] [WP].

## 7. Awards and the "indie" controversy

- **TGA 2023:** nominated **Best Independent Game**; lost to *Sea of Stars*. Critics objected because Mintrocket is part
  of Nexon. Nominees are picked by a 100+ media jury, no submissions; Geoff Keighley called it "a fair debate" [Automaton]
  [GoNintendo] [WP]. VERIFIED (secondary).
- Hwang (4Gamer via VGC, 2 Jul 2025): "we're making a game at Nexon, so there's nothing indie about it." "We never
  intended to give [Dave the Diver] an indie game feel." "But we didn't apply for it." [VGC25] VERIFIED.
- Golden Joysticks 2023 noms (Best Indie, PC GOTY, trailer); Steam Awards 2023 win; DICE 2024 three noms; GDC Awards
  2024 noms (GOTY, Best Debut, Audience); **BAFTA 2024 win: Game Design** [WP]. VERIFIED (secondary).

## A. "The remake's impossible job" (CHOSEN)

- **Hook (0.2 s):** l51 "A silent character once left space for your imagination." On frame 1 the silent hero faces
  us with an empty speech bubble "..." and the title band THE REMAKE'S / IMPOSSIBLE JOB. Within 2 s the viewer
  wonders who is going to fill that bubble.
- **Lines (episode order inside each segment, 3 segments, 2 cuts):**
  1. l51-l53 (129.12-137.75): the silent hero, "Give that character a voice... and suddenly somebody has to decide
     what that silence sounded like."
  2. l42-l50 (111.85-129.12): "Because better is measurable. More polygons. Better lighting. Better animation. Better
     sound. But familiar is not measurable. An empty field in 1998 could feel enormous. Rebuild it too literally
     today... and it might just feel empty."
  3. l54-l62 (137.75-158.45): the camera you fought with, "Fix it... and the game becomes easier to inhabit. Which is
     good. Probably." "But every improvement quietly changes the memory attached to it. That is the impossible job.
     Make it different enough to justify existing... without making people wonder where their game went."
- **Length:** 46.6 s of voice + 3.0 s wordmark/end card = **49.6 s**.
- **Why it holds a scroller:** a concrete, surprising picture in the first second (a hero whose bubble is empty) that
  touches the remake's real news (voices); a fast list with a visible "upgrade" on every word (bars fill, the field
  sharpens); a comic beat with anticipation and hold ("Which is good." / "Probably."); a clear payoff that names the
  dilemma ("the impossible job") and an open ending ("where their game went") that the full episode answers.
  Visuals reuse the episode's approved ideas for these lines (blocks F-B, G, H) re-staged for 9:16.

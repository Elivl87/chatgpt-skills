## Readings
1. **The Zelda Shorts get reach** (~1K each from the Shorts feed) and the farming ones did not (20–89). The 57–75 %
   stay rates of the EP001 Shorts come from tiny, friendly samples; the real baseline is the Zelda pair.
2. **Short #2: shorter works, the opening does not.** 23 s doubled the share watched (65.6 % vs 35.4 %), but only
   16.5 % stayed (Short #1: 28 %). Frames 0–3.5 s: a small split-screen "select" card with neutral faces → wide temple
   shot, Quest small and idle → **2.0–3.0 s an empty pedestal with no character** → Triforce only at ~3 s. The first
   line ("Player one already knows everything") is a statement with no conflict. The recognisable Zelda icon arrives
   after most people have swiped.
3. **Both critics passed Short #2; the audience didn't.** The critics are calibrated on craft and on "something changes
   every second", not on "would a stranger stop on frame 0". Recalibrated (see the retention critic skill).
4. **EP002 long is invisible, not bad:** 119 impressions in 5 days (EP001: 2,866), but 55 % average viewed and 4.2 % CTR
   on what it got. 2,000 Short views sent almost nobody to it. Distribution, not quality, is the EP002 problem.
5. Subscribers: 4 in total, 3 of them from the Zelda Shorts.

## Rules taken
- Frame 0 of a Short = a **close-up face with a strong emotion + the recognisable icon of the game** (Triforce, Deku
  Tree, tractor…) **+ a conflict in words** (a question or a contradiction), readable at feed size. A "select screen" or
  a wide shot is not a frame 0.
- The first line states a **conflict**, never a neutral fact.
- Keep 20–25 s.
- The critics' PASS is checked against real stay rates every time a Short is published; a gap means the rubric changes.

# What EP001 and EP002 taught us (2026-10-17, from `CHANNEL_STATS_2026-10-15.md`)

1. **Packaging decides distribution; the video decides retention.** EP001 was tested on Home (2,866 impressions, 91 %
   browse) with "Game: Why Millions + contradiction" and a big-face thumbnail. EP002 read as remake news and Home never
   tested it (119 impressions). Title formula from now on: game name first + "Why (millions)…" + a contradiction.
2. **The scripts work; length matters.** EP002 kept 55 % on 6:53 vs EP001's 19 % on 9:46. The director method (thesis,
   humour, "So, why?", hook contract) is right. Target 6–8 minutes; every block has to earn its time.
3. **Topic = big audience + a human question, not news.** Zelda brings reach (Shorts ~1K each vs 20–89 for farming), but
   a rumour/remake angle limits the long video to search. Pick games people already talk about and an evergreen question.
4. **Shorts find people but don't send them to the long video** (2,000 Short views → ~1 long view, 3 subs). Shorts are
   for discovery and testing hooks; the long video is the channel. Every Short: pinned comment + related video.
5. **Shorts lose people at seconds 1–3.** Frame 0 = close-up face with emotion + the game's icon + a conflict in words.
   20–25 s keeps those who stay (65.6 % watched).
6. **Our critics must be checked against reality.** Both passed Short #2; 16.5 % stayed. Every publication is compared
   with its critic verdict and the rubric changes when they disagree.
7. **Samples are still small** (2,812 views, 4 subs): these are directions, not laws. Re-read at EP003 + EP004.

## Retention beats

- **Frame 0 (partial PASS).** Close-up face: yes. Game icon: rowboat gunwale + fish visible, small (bottom ~15 % of the
  frame). Big text before narration: yes (voice starts 0.70). But (a) the emotion is a mild side-eye, not a strong one;
  the sweat/wide-eyed face used at 8.8 s reads far stronger at feed size; (b) the **conflict lives only in the banner**
  ("It's eight dollars. Just get it."), whose text is ~7 px tall at 270 px feed width: unreadable. The big text is a
  recognition claim, not a conflict/question. The channel rule asks for conflict in readable words.
- **Hook 0–2.5 s (PASS).** Line + banner highlight land together by 2.49 s; a stranger gets "friend being nagged to
  buy a game". The game is never named, which is fine for this universal concept (optional idea below).
- **Open loop:** "which friend?" opened at 0.0, closed at 8.0 ("I'll get it later.") and paid at 10.7. Good, but it
  closes at 10.7 and **nothing new is opened** for the remaining 9.7 s.
- **Escalation 2.5–10.7 (PASS):** a change every 0.5–1.0 s; three punches each bigger; weakest spot 6.5–7.5 (only
  typing dots for ~1.2 s).
- **Payoff (PASS):** "He will not get it later." on a hard cut to a stronger face + fish deadpan, one tick. Lands.
- **After payoff (FIX):** h08 "Getting a few adults into the same boat is harder than anything in the game." is a
  reflective, lower-energy line, played over a wide where nobody does anything and Leo is not identifiable. 3.4 s at
  score 1 (12.0–15.6). Anticlimax right after the laugh.
- **End card (FIX):** 3.8 s (limit ~2.5 s); last 1.9 s fully static and silent (score 0). "TAG YOUR **LEO**": the name
  Leo is never spoken; it appears only as a tiny chat label, so a first-time viewer doesn't know who Leo is.
- **Loop (FIX in effect):** the seam itself is well built, but it sits behind a dead end card, so most viewers never
  reach it. The concept's own loop (the ask restarts) only works if the last thing before the seam is energy.
- **Captions:** one line, top third, never over faces; readable. OK.

## Optional ideas (bigger, Producer's call)
- **Reorder the locked lines (verbatim, no rewording)** so the payoff is the last line before the loop:
  "It's eight dollars. Just get it." → "Bought. Bought. Bought." → "Getting a few adults into the same boat is harder
  than anything in the game." (over the wide with the three looking at Leo) → "And one friend who says 'I'll get it

  re-order of blocks 3–5. If taken, fixes 2 and 1 become simpler.

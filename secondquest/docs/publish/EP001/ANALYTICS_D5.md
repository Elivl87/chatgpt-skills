## Lessons (update to the 48 h ones)

1. **The first 30 seconds decide distribution.** At 48 h, 60 % were still watching at 0:30. With a wider Home audience,
   only 38 % are. YouTube widened the test and then stopped pushing (views flat since ~day 3.5). The thumbnail's promise
   must be on screen **in the first 5–10 s**, not at 0:30, and the opening must look like the thumbnail.
2. **Home matters more than search for us now** (96 % Browse). The title that shows first should be the one that makes a
   stranger on Home click: curiosity first, keyword still at the start where it reads naturally. This questions the
   current rule "publish with title A (search), test with B" (PUBLISHING_STANDARD §3.6). Proposed change, for the
   Producer to decide: publish with the curiosity title and test the search title.
3. **The middle slides; the ending pulls.** Rewatch bumps sit where something concrete and visual happens (the tractors),
   and the closing line pulls people back. Every ~60–90 s needs a concrete, visual beat; the closing works.
4. **Shorts: a concrete, extreme claim wins the feed.** The tractor Short ("costs more than a house": a number, a
   contrast) got 86.5 % of its views from the Shorts feed. The first Short (a softer question) barely entered the feed
   and lived on search. 43 % swipe away at once, so **the first 1–2 seconds must be the hook line itself**, not a build-up.
5. **Shorts have not yet brought subscribers or episode views.** Next Shorts: set the full episode as the **related
   video** (§10), end with the card, and test a direct "the full story is on the channel" line.

  in the room (0:00), the ocarina, sword and Triforce come at 0:09–0:17, and "You." at 0:21. A thumbnail built on the
  **1998 room / CRT / cartridge** (options 1 and 2) pays off visually from frame one. Options 4–5 pay off late.
- **Title:** the curiosity version goes up first (see lesson 2), with "Ocarina of Time" at the start.
- **Trailer Short (S1):** open on the hook line itself in the first second ("There is one thing Nintendo cannot rebuild…
  You."), then the anchors, ending on "So, why?". This is an edit of approved lines, with no new words.

# TikTok plan v1 (2026-10-17)

## Principles (sources: Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Sendible, SociaLync 2026 guides; vendor numbers unverified)
- New accounts are shown first to a small non-follower test audience; watch time / completion, shares and saves drive
  expansion. The first 1–2 s decide everything (same lesson as our YouTube Shorts: frame 0 = face + icon + conflict).
- One clear topic per video; post consistently (3–5 / week to start).
- Length: 20–45 s for completion; ~30–60 s for total watch time. Our Shorts (23–60 s) fit.
- Upload the clean master (never a YouTube-watermarked download). Use TikTok's own text/captions where possible.
- AI label: TikTok requires it for realistic AI people/scenes; our 2D cartoon with a stock narrator is outside that
  scope per secondary sources. If unsure, switch the AI toggle on: it does not reduce reach (per the same sources).
- Rights: no Nintendo audio on TikTok (the EP002 Short #1 uses the Producer's original Navi clips → mute risk).

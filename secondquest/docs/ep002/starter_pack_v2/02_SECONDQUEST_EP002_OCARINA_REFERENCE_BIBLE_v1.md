# SECONDQUEST — EP002
## OCARINA OF TIME REFERENCE BIBLE v1
### Production-focused — not a lore encyclopedia

## Current remake facts relevant to EP002
Nintendo currently lists **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time** for Nintendo Switch 2 with release date **November 5, 2026**. Nintendo describes it as **built from the ground up** for Switch 2, with completely overhauled visuals, voiced cutscenes, expanded dialogue, symphonic orchestral music, enhanced controls, jump/dash actions and modern camera movement.

Primary source: https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-switch-2/

Production implication: the script's “technical modernization vs familiarity” conflict is directly supported by Nintendo's current framing.

## Navi
Original-development interviews explain that a targeting marker became a fairy-based navigation system and was named Navi from “navigation.” Navi grew from a system into a guide and story presence.
Sources:
- https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/1/3/
- https://www.nintendo.com/es-es/Iwata-pregunta/Iwata-pregunta-The-Legend-of-Zelda-Ocarina-of-Time-3D/Vol-2-El-equipo-de-desarrollo-del-original-primera-parte/4-El-origen-del-nombre-Navi-/4-El-origen-del-nombre-Navi--231748.html

**EP002:** Navi is mandatory with Quest inside Ocarina and functions as identity + motion + depth + eye guidance + transition + temporal continuity.

## Young / Adult Hero
Ocarina's child/adult structure is central to EP002. Young Quest / Adult Quest are not cosmetic variants. They are the episode's core visual metaphor for time, memory, growth and return. Reuse camera axes and locations across ages so change is visible.

## Epona
Nintendo's development interviews describe riding Epona as a distinctive characteristic of Ocarina and discuss the attention given to horse presentation/camera.
Sources:
- https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/1/4/
- https://www.nintendo.com/es-es/Iwata-pregunta/Iwata-pregunta-The-Legend-of-Zelda-Ocarina-of-Time-3D/Vol-2-El-equipo-de-desarrollo-del-original-primera-parte/5-Lo-que-no-pudimos-hacer-en-Ocarina-of-Time/5-Lo-que-no-pudimos-hacer-en-Ocarina-of-Time-231818.html

**EP002:** Epona belongs to the adult arc. Best use: Young Quest + Navi cross Hyrule on foot → Adult Quest + Navi + Epona cross the same road. This embodies **Same road. Different person.**

## Saria
Saria's production function is childhood friendship and the emotional world Young Quest leaves behind. Do not explain her biography. Veterans recognize her; newcomers should understand closeness from staging.

## Princess Zelda
Use Zelda primarily in the returning-player knowledge sequence. She helps communicate accumulated recognition around Ocarina / Triforce / fate. Do not create a separate Zelda subplot. Sheik is excluded.

## Ganondorf
Nintendo's current remake page frames Ganondorf as seeking the Triforce from the Sacred Realm. Use him concisely in the returning-player knowledge sequence and the “very bad decision” beat. No villain exposition is needed.

## Great Deku Tree
Required for the new-player joke. The audience should clearly read **Great Deku Tree**, not “large fantasy tree.” Keep staging serious and let Bram's line create the humor.

## Ocarina of Time — object
Titular, musical, interactive and tied to the game's time/world language. Use as recognition anchor, memory object and transition symbol. Navi can guide the viewer toward it. Never treat it as a generic blue flute.

## Master Sword
Primary EP002 meaning: **threshold into adulthood/time.** Its major use belongs to Temple of Time + pedestal + child→adult transition. Do not use it everywhere merely because it looks heroic.

## Triforce
The script explicitly references three golden triangles and wisdom/power/courage. Use it directly there. Do not make it wallpaper.

## Hylian Shield
Part of Adult Hero-of-Time Quest's readable silhouette. It does not need an explanatory beat. Use only when it strengthens recognition without cluttering emotion.

## Nintendo 64 + original cartridge
EP002 framing device. Hook: physical artifact of 1998. Act 5/Closing: physical game still exists. Creates the loop **physical game → remembered world → physical game → remembered world meets adult player.** Use recognizable real-console/cartridge language, not fictional retro hardware.

## Hyrule Field
Primary emotional geography. Use for scale, two audiences, child/adult comparison, Epona, Same Road and final hero image. It should evolve with the story rather than reappear as a copied background.

## Kokiri / forest language
Represents childhood, beginning, Saria and early memory. Emotional counterpoint to Hyrule Field.

## Temple of Time
Principal architectural language for threshold, Master Sword, child/adult transformation and time. Must be clearly recognizable in the temporal hero sequence.

## Hero-of-Time Quest
Quest is never replaced by Link. The concept is **Quest inhabits Link's recognizable heroic role.** Preserve Quest face/identity. Make the role/outfit unmistakable and sincere, not a cosplay gag.

## Explicitly excluded
Do not use Sheik, Impa, Darunia, Ruto, Nabooru, Rauru, Malon or a Sages montage/group. They are valid Ocarina characters but do not improve this seven-minute argument enough to justify density/cost.

## Principle
A recognizable element appears because it advances story, strengthens emotion, rewards recognition, clarifies Ocarina identity or helps retention. If it serves none of those, omit it.

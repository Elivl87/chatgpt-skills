# SECONDQUEST — EP002
## CREATIVE PRODUCTION DIRECTIVE v2

## Purpose
EP002 is a cinematic visual essay about memory, returning, childhood, adulthood, what remakes can improve, what remakes cannot reproduce, and how the player changes even when the game remains recognizable.

It is **not** a lore recap, character encyclopedia, or fan-service checklist. The emotional argument is: **You cannot remake the exact memory — but a remake can let the game you remember meet the person you became.**

## Viewer-first
Every decision is judged by retention, curiosity, emotional connection, clarity, surprise, visual pleasure, recognition, pacing, payoff and desire to watch another SecondQuest episode. Production convenience is secondary. Retention does not mean constant cuts: silence, restraint, breathing room and emotional holds are valid tools.

## SecondQuest identity
Repeat the system, not the shot formulas. Quest, the WHY identity, cinematic essay structure and channel signatures can recur; identical compositions, transitions, pacing and hero-shot formulas should not. EP002's grammar is **memory + time + same place/different person + child/adult echoes + physical 1998 artifacts + unmistakable Ocarina vocabulary + Navi as connective motion + Hyrule Field as emotional geography.**

## Script authority
SCRIPT v3 is locked. Do not add lore exposition or character biographies. The script says the idea; the direction makes it unmistakably Ocarina.

## Ocarina identity — non-negotiable
When recognition matters, use clear references: Nintendo 64 + original Ocarina cartridge, Ocarina of Time, Master Sword, Triforce, Hylian Shield language, Hero-of-Time Quest treatment, Navi, Epona, Saria, Zelda, Ganondorf, Great Deku Tree, Temple of Time, Kokiri/forest language and Hyrule Field. Do not replace these with generic fantasy approximations. If a required exact reference cannot be solved from approved art, flag the gap instead of weakening it.

## Quest transformation — locked
Real world: normal Quest_v1.
Entry: Navi appears and guides Quest through the threshold.
Inside Ocarina: Quest adopts a sincere Hero-of-Time-inspired role treatment while remaining recognizably Quest. Young and Adult Quest must feel like temporal states of the same character.
Exit: Quest returns to normal SecondQuest outfit.

## Navi — locked
Inside Ocarina, Navi accompanies Quest unless the Director specifies an exception. She is a cinematic system: eye guidance, depth cue, transitions, reveals, child→adult continuity and subtle life in held frames. Do not park her at the same shoulder position in every shot.

## Excluded characters
Do not use Sheik, Impa, Darunia, Ruto, Nabooru, Rauru, Malon or a Sages-group montage. Do not hide them in backgrounds.

## Storyboard — Runway only
Runway is the storyboard executor. Higgsfield is not used for storyboard exploration. The storyboard is rough, cheap, temporary and internal. It can never be inserted, animated, cropped, upscaled, stored as canon or placed in the production art library. Even if a storyboard frame looks excellent, **do not use it in the video.**

## Minimum art, maximum editorial value
Do not create one artwork per narration sentence. Build visual families and base plates that can produce multiple editorial shots via crop, pan, push, parallax, focus, foreground reveal, lighting change, silhouette, match cuts and programmatic overlays. **Shot count is not art count.**

## Production order
1. REUSE
2. RECOMPOSE
3. PROGRAMMATIC
4. DERIVE
5. economical missing background if justified
6. NEW_ART

Reuse never means using an irrelevant asset, repeating a pose until the episode feels copy-pasted, hiding required Ocarina references, or weakening a hero moment.

## Build before you buy
After storyboard review, inspect the real library and actually mount the directed scenes. Show real production reconstructions **lámina by lámina** using actual assets. Do not merely report “REUSE possible.”

## NEW_ART gate
Only after real reconstruction may a true NEW_ART gap be proposed. Each asset must name the exact sequence(s), what materially fails without it, why REUSE/RECOMPOSE/PROGRAMMATIC/DERIVE fail, how many scenes it serves, expected generations and estimated Higgsfield credits. Then STOP for Eli's approval.

## Official marks/logo
The official Ocarina title/logo may be used as a restrained editorial identifier when directed and when a correct approved source asset is available. Do not wallpaper the video with it or use it as SecondQuest branding. The cartridge label or one title identifier may be enough.

## Audio
Current approved state: **MUSIC OFF.** Do not add music unless Eli explicitly enables the optional plan.

## Final creative test
Before a scene is ready: Does it serve the viewer? deepen narration? feel SecondQuest? read as Ocarina when required? preserve Quest? use Navi intentionally? reuse coherently? protect hero moments? extract enough value from its base plate? Is NEW_ART genuinely necessary?

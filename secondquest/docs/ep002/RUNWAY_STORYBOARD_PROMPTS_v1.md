# SecondQuest EP002: Runway storyboard prompts v1

**Use:** planning only. **Storyboard material NEVER enters the video, the art library or canon** (Runway Storyboard Brief v1).

**Settings in Runway**
- Image generation, 16:9, highest available resolution.
- Each prompt stays under ~1,000 characters, Runway's typical text limit.
- Optional: attach the SecondQuest avatar (`docs/publish/brand/SecondQuest_avatar_800.png`) as a character reference so Quest reads correctly.
- Do one or two attempts per board. Do not polish faces or hands.

**Plan:**
1. Try the **MASTER** board first (one image, 30 panels).
2. If its panels are too small or muddled, use the **6 grouped boards (A–F)** instead (5 panels each, still cheap).

Runway renders text poorly, so it only adds panel numbers. Claude maps every panel to its sequence, script line and production label afterwards.

## MASTER — 30 panels, one image

```
Rough storyboard contact sheet, 6 columns x 5 rows, 30 numbered panels, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, white gutters, film previsualization, no polish. Story of Quest, young man with dark brown curly hair and red hoodie. Panels 1-4: Quest in a 90s bedroom inserts a game cartridge into a Nintendo 64; a glowing blue fairy appears; he follows it into a fantasy green field and his clothes become a green tunic hero outfit; close-ups of a blue ocarina, a sacred sword, three golden triangles. Panels 5-12: child Quest by an old CRT TV, tiny child hero in a vast field, forest friend girl with green hair, technical wireframe overlays. Panels 13-20: same field seen twice, giant ancient tree with a face, princess, dark armored king, museum glass, temple with sword in pedestal, child becomes adult. Panels 21-30: back to the N64 room, adult hero riding a horse on the same road, empty bedroom, final shot adult hero beside his horse at sunset with the fairy.
```

## Grouped boards (use if the MASTER is not readable)

Every board uses 5 panels in one row: rough pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, numbered.

**Quest in all boards:** dark brown curly hair, freckles. Real world: red hoodie. Inside the game: green tunic hero outfit (child or adult version), always with a small glowing blue fairy companion.

### A — Hook / Entry (sequences 01–04)

```
Rough storyboard, 5 numbered panels in a row, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, film previs. 1: young man with dark brown curly hair and red hoodie in a dim 90s bedroom holds a grey game cartridge beside a Nintendo 64 console, old CRT TV glowing. 2: he inserts the cartridge; a small glowing blue fairy flies out of the TV light. 3: he steps after the fairy through bright light into a green fantasy field; his hoodie turns into a green tunic hero outfit. 4: three close-up insets: a blue ocarina, a sword with a purple hilt, three golden triangles. 5: the green-tunic hero seen from behind facing a vast field with a distant castle, fairy ahead, wide still frame.
```

### B — Childhood / Memory (sequences 05–09)

```
Rough storyboard, 5 numbered panels in a row, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, film previs. 1: a child with dark curly hair sits on a bedroom floor in front of an old CRT TV, afternoon light, an empty cushion where a friend sat. 2: a child hero in a green tunic with a glowing blue fairy beside a girl with short green hair in a sunny forest village. 3: extreme wide shot, the tiny child hero alone in an enormous green field, huge sky, castle far away. 4: same field covered by technical wireframe polygons and resolution labels. 5: continuous path from forest edge across the field toward a distant castle, fairy leading.
```

### C — Remake / Familiarity (sequences 10–12)

```
Rough storyboard, 5 numbered panels in a row, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, film previs. 1: a green-tunic hero with a glowing blue fairy stands in a field while the world around him is redrawn more detailed. 2: same frame covered in meters and gauges for lighting, polygons, sound. 3: same frame with every meter removed, only the hero, fairy and field. 4: same field shown as a soft warm memory beside a sharp literal modern version. 5: the hero framed rigidly by an old fixed camera border, then the border dissolves into a free camera angle.
```

### D — Two Audiences (sequences 13–16)

```
Rough storyboard, 5 numbered panels in a row, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, film previs. 1: one field seen by two viewers, one with fresh eyes, one with faint memory echoes. 2: chain of icons: a blue ocarina, a princess in a pink gown, a sword in a stone pedestal, three golden triangles. 3: a dark armored villain king reaching for three golden triangles. 4: a tiny green-tunic child hero with a glowing fairy before a gigantic ancient tree with a solemn face. 5: the same field, half clean, half overlaid with faint ghosts of a child, a bedroom and a forest girl.
```

### E — Time (sequences 17–20)

```
Rough storyboard, 5 numbered panels in a row, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, film previs. 1: a small fantasy world preserved under museum glass, sharp and still. 2: the glass cracks open, air and light flow in. 3: a flat 2D map unfolds into deep 3D space, a glowing fairy flies from foreground to background. 4: inside a stone temple a child hero in a green tunic with a fairy stands before a sword in a pedestal, fixed camera. 5: identical framing, now an adult hero in a green tunic with a shield stands in the same spot, same fairy.
```

### F — Return / Growth / Ending (sequences 21–30)

```
Rough storyboard, 5 numbered panels in a row, loose pencil and grey marker sketches with small color accents, film previs. 1: a young man in a red hoodie holds an old game cartridge beside a Nintendo 64 in a bedroom. 2: a child hero walks a long road across a field on foot, matching shot: an adult hero rides a horse on the same road. 3: the adult hero stops and stands beside his horse, faint echo of the child far behind. 4: the same bedroom, empty, a TV glowing, nobody there. 5: final wide shot at sunset, adult green-tunic hero standing beside his horse with a small glowing fairy, calm, looking at the horizon.
```

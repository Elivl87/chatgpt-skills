animatic with `scripts/pacing_qc.py docs/style/<name>.json`. Raw numbers: `docs/style/*.json`, contact sheets `*_sheet.jpg`.

## What the numbers say about EP003 v3
The animatic holds shots almost twice as long as EP002 and moves half as much. That is the "static" feeling: too few
cuts, cut-outs that do not move, water and sky that are still. Fixes, all free: split the long shots (the 36 s one is
D4/E), add idle motion (water ripples, clouds, bob, blinks, lantern flicker), eased entrances with overshoot, and
reaction cutaways (the fish companion is the natural one).

## Picture DNA (EP001/EP002)
Painted 2D plates and characters (generated, never edited), ink outlines on 3D props, warm palette (browns, golds,
sky blue), the wordmark with the gold bar on "So, why?" + 1.5 s, cards in our ui_kit families, HUD only in the game world.

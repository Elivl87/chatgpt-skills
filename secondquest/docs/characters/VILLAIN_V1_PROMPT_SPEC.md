## Identity block (every prompt that shows him)
```
A cartoon villain character of our own design: an imposing, towering warlord king of evil, very tall, very broad
shoulders, heavy dark armour in charcoal and deep bronze plates with no emblems, a long dark tattered cape, a deep hood
that casts his face into shadow: two glowing red eyes and a small glowing amber oval gem on his forehead (our own
design), strands of fiery red hair escaping from the hood, dark skin. 2D cartoon style with bold heavy ink outlines,
flat cel shading, a darker palette. Menacing but family friendly: no blood, no weapons.
```
- **Inspired, never a replica:** no third-party names in prompts, no copied armour pattern, gem or face.
- **Staging:** low angle, he towers over Quest; heavier inks than Quest and Pixie.
- **Unhooded face:** not designed yet (Producer chose hooded only). Designing it is a separate, quoted step.
- **In the engine/animatic:** as a shadow he is graded violet-black with only the eyes and the gem glowing
  (`scripts/ep002-blockI-animatic.py` `_villain_shade`).

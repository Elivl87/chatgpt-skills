## 4. What made it land (critics and players)

- **A goodbye as the opening.** Players and critics single out the prologue (Gustave and Sophie at the harbour) and
  the Act 1 death. Inverse's Hayes Madsen wrote about the game's grief through **the loss of his own brother**.
  VERIFIED (inverse.com/gaming/expedition-33-twist-gustave-clair-obscur).
- **Turn-based with your hands.** Parry and dodge make a menu game physical. Critics frame it as "the turn-based heir to
  classic Final Fantasy" (Gfinity), "a gorgeous new tomorrow for RPGs, built upon yesterday" (Destructoid headline) and
  "deeply satisfying homage to Japanese role-playing games" (The Guardian headline). VERIFIED (headlines).
- **The music.** It is a breakout hit (NME feature "How the soundtrack became a breakout smash hit"). VERIFIED.
- **The underdog.** A team of about 30 under Ubisoft's shadow sweeps the awards. Polygon: "astonishing Game of the Year
  run should change gaming for the better" (26 Apr 2026, headline). VERIFIED (secondary).
- **Charlie Cox's Gustave.** A viral post praising his performance got 813k views and 26k likes in a day (Know Your
  Meme). VERIFIED (secondary).

**What this means (for the Producer's decision; the coordinator is doing the risk check)**
- **Music:** the realistic outcomes of using official cues are a Content ID claim (ads go to the rights holder, which
  doesn't matter here), a **mute or territorial block** if the owner chose "block", or rarely a manual takedown
  (strike). Short excerpts under commentary are the common fan-video practice, but **fair use is not guaranteed**.
  Safest faithful route: **write to Sandfall/Kepler for written permission** (a personal, non-monetized tribute is a
  sympathetic ask), then upload **private first** and read Checks before going public.
- **Art:** "faithful" art of the characters (Gustave, Maelle, Verso, the Paintress) is the high-risk category in

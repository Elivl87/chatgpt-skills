## ERRORS (must fix)
1. **¶41 camp piano duet.** Nothing in the transcript shows Maelle playing with Verso at camp. Maelle rel. lvl 4 has only talk: Verso: "Before the Fracture, I studied piano at the Conservatory." Maelle: "…we can fix up the opera house and you can perform for us." The famous piano scene is **Maelle's ending**: everyone gathers to watch Verso play in concert (Zappia). The fan wiki is the only source for the duet, so it is not safe to state. See addition 1.
2. **¶54 "One year at a time"** puts the yearly pace on Renoir. In the game the yearly number is Aline's resistance. Alicia's letter (Monolith) calls her "the sole force standing against the one who would erase us", her power is waning, and she can save only a few. As written, ¶54 contradicts ¶46.
3. **¶56 "the weapon you used on the Paintress".** The Curator forged a weapon from Axon hearts "to pierce the barrier" protecting her (Wikipedia). → "Even the weapon that broke her barrier."

## RISKY
- **¶62/¶64:** both lines come from the epilogue *A Life to Paint* (Maelle's ending), after the choice. Fans will recognise them. Frame them so the timing is honest ("Later, she'll tell him…"), or tag them on screen.
- **¶24 "a creature from a children's story":** the transcript only says "In the stories, they say Esquie likes to speak in riddles" (Sciel). → "a creature out of Lumière's old stories."
- **¶23 "he meets Esquie":** the whole party (Lune, Maelle, Sciel) is there. ¶31 is also Maelle's first mention, and she arrives with no bond. Maelle: "It's why he and his sister took me in."
- **¶81 "so she stopped everything":** this tips Aline toward villain. The transcript says the canvas "was killing… our mother". → "so she stayed inside, and it was killing her."
- **¶36 "hands you his sword":** it is ambiguous whose sword. → "hands you Verso instead."
- **¶13 "at the harbor… she says":** she says it before the harbour gathering. Drop "at the harbor" or "word for word".
- **¶70 "a family… at a grave":** the transcript does not confirm the grave. Recheck in game.
- **Monoco is never named**, and no other cut will annoy fans as much. A single clause in ¶70 would fix it ("Lune. Sciel. Monoco.").

## Top 3 additions / corrections
1. **CURRENT ¶41** "Verso at the piano. Maelle sits down and plays too."
   → **PROPOSED** "Verso tells Maelle he studied piano before the Fracture. She says: when this is over, you'll play for us at the opera house." Then pay it off at **¶67–¶69**: "And he does. Everyone's there. Gustave. Sophie. And Verso plays… because she won't let him stop."
   → **REASON** The duet is not attested. The real promise and its payoff are verified and well loved, and they make Maelle's ending beautiful and wrong in the same image.
2. **CURRENT ¶54** "The only way to do it… was to erase the painting. One year at a time."
   → **PROPOSED** "He said it himself: 'For the sake of the living, we must part with the dead.' So he erased it. And every year, she held him back a little less. That's the number." (Renoir's line is verified in Back to Lumière.)
   → **REASON** This fixes error 2, and it gives Renoir his own words, the same balance the script already gives Maelle and Verso.
3. **CURRENT ¶31** "Gustave steps in front of Maelle, sixteen…"
   → **PROPOSED** "…Maelle, sixteen, the girl he and his sister took in…"
   → **REASON** Fans feel Gustave's death through this bond. It also gives ¶60 ("the girl Gustave died for") its weight.

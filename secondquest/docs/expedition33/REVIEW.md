# Expedition 33 · Script package review (stage 2)

**Method, disclosed.** The brief asked for up to 4 reviewer subagents. This session has **no subagent tool**, so the four
lenses below were run by the writer as **separate, sequential passes**, one lens at a time, re-reading each full
script against its checklist (VOICE.md, PUBLISHING_STANDARD, THUMBNAIL_RULES, MONETIZATION_SAFETY_STANDARD, LEARNINGS,
RESEARCH ids). They are not independent. **Before lock, the coordinator should run 4 independent reviewers** on the
chosen package (same briefs) and a Spanish-naturalness reviewer on its ES copy.

Lenses: (1) hook / first 30 s · (2) retention and pacing · (3) originality + fact accuracy · (4) IP + title/thumbnail
promise. The Producer's rules applied during this review were 8–10 min, FULL SPOILER with warnings, the motto as the
closing argument (grief → legacy → letting go → life goes on), and the faithful music/art plan kept separate from the narration.

## Recommendation: **A · "The Painting That Wouldn't Let Go"**

A and B tie on points. A wins on the four things that decide this episode:
1. **It is the Producer's argument, start to finish.** Grief → the four ways a family grieves → the choice → letting
   go → the family alive at a grave. The binding motto rule is A's spine, not its ending.
2. **Packaging.** "Expedition 33: Why Millions Love a Game About Losing Everyone" is the EP001 formula with the
   sharpest contradiction of the three. Its thumbnail (held-back tears + the "33" tower + ONE LAST MINUTE) reads at
   phone size and spoils nothing.
3. **It is made to be shared.** "Every Dessendre grieves differently, and you've met all of them" is the line people
   send to someone they lost someone with. That is the "remember forever" bar the Producer set.
4. **The music plan fits best.** The father's theme sits under the father's choice (block D), and "Une vie à
   t'aimer" carries the climb from "why it hits" to the grave, then goes silent for the motto.

**B's two best beats** (the arithmetic and "the one person who wants his number to come") could serve as the
**trailer Short** for A without changing A's script.

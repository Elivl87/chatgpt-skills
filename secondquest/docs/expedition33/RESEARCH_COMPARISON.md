## Verdict
They complement each other, but the Producer's dossier is better where it matters most for this episode: it explains
*why people cared*, which is what a script is built from. The agent's research is a fact sheet; it answers "is this true
and can we use it?" but not "what did it mean to people?".

## What the agent got wrong for the script
- Package A ends on the family letting go at the grave: that frames Verso's ending as the answer. The dossier asks not
  to crown either ending and to give Maelle's choice a serious argument.
- Destination: "letting go" alone; the dossier's stronger destination is LEGACY / continuation: "don't disappear with
  them", grief that keeps memory without stopping tomorrow.
- Too little life and lightness (Esquie, camp, piano): constant sadness flattens the hits.
- Gustave and Renoir underweighted; Aline at risk of being a "villain who couldn't let go".

## Fix to the process (applied)
`.claude/skills/secondquest-episode/SKILL.md` stage 1 now requires an **Audience resonance dossier** next to the fact
sheet (model: `PRODUCER_RESEARCH_DOSSIER.txt`), and the writer must use both.

## Why the paint drop
- **It is the motto with eyes.** Everywhere it goes, it leaves a line of paint behind it, a trail for whoever comes
  next. At the end it paints FOR THOSE WHO COME AFTER, the only time the trail becomes words. The essay's last turn
  (legacy, letting go) is something it has been doing all along.
- **It's spoiler-safe and pays off the spoiler.** Before the reveal it is a cute painterly touch; after "It's a
  painting", the audience realises the companion was a clue from the first frame.
- **It works in all three packages** (grief, deadline, legacy) and has a natural expression set: round eyes (curious),
  squashed flat (scared of the giant brush), stretched tall (proud), a single drip (sad), running thin (tired).
- **IP-clean:** a paint drop is not any of the game's creatures (Gestrals, Esquie, Mimes); its only link is the white
  paint of the numbers, a motif and not a design.
